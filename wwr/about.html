Xtreme Fun

At Xtreme Fun, we believe every rapid tells a story. Our mission is to deliver unforgettable whitewater adventures while prioritizing safety, teamwork, and respect for nature

History

Xtreme Fun was founded in 2015 by a group of passionate river guides who wanted to share their love for whitewater adventure with the world. What started as a small local operation has grown into one of Bolivia's most trusted rafting companies.
